Privacy Policy for ALL AT A1

Last Updated: March 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ALL AT A1. We are committed to providing a secure and private environment for students, job seekers, and recruiters. This policy outlines our commitment to your privacy across our blogs, educational resources, and job services.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in absolute transparency and minimal data collection. We only collect information that is voluntarily provided by you through our direct interaction endpoints:

  • Contact Inquiries: When you use our "Contact Us" form, we securely receive your name, email address, and message exclusively to provide you with customer support and direct responses.
  • Recruiter Submissions (Post a Job): When you post a job, we collect necessary professional details, including your Company Name, Admin Email, and Website URLs. This is strictly required to verify the authenticity of the listing, power our AI Job Poster generation text, and prevent automated spam on the platform.
  • Research Desk Submissions: When you submit a complex technical query to our Research Desk, we collect your name, email ID, and the contextual details of your research topic so that our engineering team receives the context needed to process your request and potentially notify you.
  • Educational Content & Blogs: Our resources for students are entirely open-access. You do not need to provide any personal information, accounts, or emails to read our blogs, roadmaps, or technical guides.

3. Our Absolute Data Protection Commitment (No Selling)

We aggressively protect the trust you place in our platform. We operate under a strict "No-Lease, No-Sale" mandate regarding your direct personal information:

  • Never Sold or Rented: We explicitly guarantee that we will NEVER sell, rent, lease, or commercially distribute your personal data (including Contact Us names, Research Desk emails, or Recruiter job posting credentials) to third-party data brokers, marketing agencies, or spam lists.
  • Recruiter Privacy: The private admin email address and internal professional details you provide during the "Post a Job" process are utilized strictly and exclusively for the backend operational management of the job portal (such as tracking your uploaded jobs or AI moderation alerts). They are heavily siloed from public view and external databases.
  • Research Data Security: While the core technical concepts of your submitted Research Topics may be published as public guides after AI generation, your personal identifying markers (like your private email ID used to submit the research request) are completely disconnected from the public output and remain highly secure on our internal servers.

4. Educational Resources for Students

Our central objective is to help students succeed. Any technical guides, research topics, or career roadmap information we provide is meant entirely for public educational benefit and exploration.

5. Advertising, Cookies, and Google AdSense

ALL AT A1 uses third-party advertising companies, specifically Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use aggregated tracking information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide targeted advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve targeted ads to our users based on their browsing history on our site and/or other sites across the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

By using our website, you explicitly consent to the use of these tracking cookies and the sharing of captured behavioral data with Google and our certified third-party advertising partners for monetization purposes.

6. Third-Party Links

While we aggregate job listings from external sites, we act only as a bridge. Clicking on these links will direct you outside our platform. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external companies, and we encourage you to check their policies once you leave our portal.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as we add more features or adjust our advertising partners. The "Last Updated" date will always show our most recent version.
