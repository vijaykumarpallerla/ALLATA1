Privacy Policy for ALL AT A1

Last Updated: March 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ALL AT A1. We are committed to providing a secure and private environment for students, job seekers, and recruiters. This policy outlines our commitment to your privacy across our blogs, educational resources, and job services.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. We only collect information that is voluntarily provided by you through our interactive features:

  • Educational Content & Blogs: Our resources for students are open-access. You do not need to provide any information to read our blogs or technical guides.
  • Contact Inquiries: When you use our "Contact Us" form, we receive your name and email address to respond to your request.
  • Recruiter Submissions: When you post a job, we collect necessary professional details (Company Name, Email, Website) to facilitate the listing.

3. Our Privacy Commitment (No Selling or Sharing)

We follow a strict "No-Lease, No-Sale" policy regarding your information:

  • Contact Data: We will NEVER sell or rent your name or contact details to third-party marketing agencies.
  • Recruiter Privacy: Your email address and professional details provided during job posting are used only for the legitimate operation of the job portal. We never share this data with third-party apps, external databases, or advertisers.
  • No Tracking: We do not use cookies or analytics scripts to track your behavior across other websites.

4. Educational Resources for Students

Our goal is to help students succeed. Any technical guides, research topics, or career roadmap information we provide is meant for public educational benefit. We do not use these interactions to profile students or build digital personas for advertising purposes.

5. Third-Party Links

While we aggregate job listings from external sites, we act only as a bridge. Clicking on these links will direct you outside our platform. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external companies, and we encourage you to check their policies once you leave our portal.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as we add more features for students and employers. The "Last Updated" date will always show our most recent version.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding your privacy, please reach out to us:

Email: allata1@googlegroups.com
